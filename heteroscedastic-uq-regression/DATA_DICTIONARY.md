# Data dictionary

The committed `results/` directory contains processed, non-confidential analysis outputs. The complete seed-, fold-, bootstrap-, sensitivity-, and prediction-level result set is regenerated by `analysis/reviewer10_final_analysis.py`.

## Committed result files

- `synthetic_ood_utility_seed.csv` — one row per optimization seed and dropout rate, containing OOD AUROC, OOD AUPRC, and Spearman correlation between epistemic SD and absolute prediction error.
- `synthetic_ood_utility_summary.csv` — mean and SD of the synthetic OOD-utility metrics across seeds.
- `diabetes_oof_summary.csv` — out-of-fold predictive accuracy, likelihood, coverage, interval-width, and Winkler summaries for the Diabetes benchmark.
- `engel_oof_summary.csv` — corresponding out-of-fold summaries for the Engel benchmark.
- `grunfeld_group_oof_summary.csv` — leave-one-firm-out Grunfeld summaries.
- `grunfeld_temporal_summary.csv` — disjoint temporal-window Grunfeld summaries.
- `compute_benchmark.csv` — measured CPU training and inference time, stored networks, parameter counts, and forward-evaluation counts.
- `software_hardware_environment.json` — Python, package, platform, CPU, device, seed, and log-variance-bound metadata.
- `pytest_log.txt` — test-suite result.

## Fields used in benchmark summaries

- `mse`: mean squared error.
- `mae`: mean absolute error.
- `r2`: coefficient of determination.
- `nll`: exact finite Gaussian-mixture negative log-likelihood for probabilistic neural models; blank for distribution-free conformal intervals.
- `picp`: empirical 95% prediction-interval coverage probability.
- `coverage_gap`: absolute difference between PICP and 0.95.
- `mpiw`: mean 95% prediction-interval width.
- `winkler`: 95% Winkler interval score.
- `rece`: regression expected calibration error.
- `ood_auroc` and `ood_auprc`: discrimination of OOD versus in-distribution observations using epistemic SD.
- `error_spearman`: Spearman association between epistemic SD and absolute prediction error.

Public benchmark data are loaded programmatically from `scikit-learn` and `statsmodels`; synthetic and simulated data are generated by the analysis script. No private participant data are distributed.
